PackTrax Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What this app does

PackTrax is a dog-walk tracker. It lets you log walks, assign them to dogs, optionally record a GPS route, and build simple invoices.

Data storage

All data you enter — dogs, walks, notes, prices, and recorded GPS routes — is stored only on your device, using your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and PackTrax has no backend or account system. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes this information permanently.

Location access

If you grant location permission, PackTrax uses your device's GPS only while you are actively tracking a walk, to draw the route and estimate distance. This location data is saved locally with that walk and is never transmitted anywhere by the app.

Sharing

PackTrax can generate a snapshot image of a walk or invoice and hand it to your device's native share sheet (for example, to send by text message) at your explicit request. This happens locally on your device; PackTrax itself does not send anything over the network.

Third-party services

Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap to display street maps. Loading a map tile sends your device's IP address and the map area being viewed to OpenStreetMap's servers, subject to their own privacy practices.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app's publisher.

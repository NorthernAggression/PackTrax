PackTrax Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

PackTrax is a tool for dog walkers and pet-care businesses to log walks, daycare and boarding, and to create invoices. This page explains what information the app handles and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. Your password is stored by Firebase in protected form and is never visible to us.
  • Information you enter: dog names and colors, walk details (date, duration, distance, notes, potty counts), daycare and boarding check-in and check-out times, invoices (dog names, dates, amounts and paid status), and your settings (business name, Venmo username, Zelle phone or email, default prices).
  • Location: if you track a walk, the app uses your phone's location only while that walk is being tracked, to record the route and distance. The route is saved with that walk.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your own account. Each account can only read and write its own data. A copy is also kept on your phone so the app opens quickly and works with a weak signal. Signing out does not delete the data stored in your account.

Third-party services

To work, the app contacts these services. They may receive technical information such as your IP address:

  • Google Firebase, for sign-in and data storage.
  • OpenStreetMap routing service (routing.openstreetmap.de): after a walk is saved, its GPS points are sent to this free service to line the route up with real streets.
  • Map tile providers (OpenStreetMap, Esri, Carto): your device downloads map images from them when a map is shown. The area being viewed is part of those requests.
  • Google Fonts, for the app's typefaces.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not share one user's data with another user.

Sharing invoices and walks

When you tap share, the app creates an image and hands it to your phone's share menu. You choose who receives it. The app does not send it anywhere itself.

Client information

PackTrax is designed around dog names. If you choose to type other information about your clients into notes or names, you are responsible for having the right to do so.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete individual dogs, walks, daycare visits, boarding stays and invoices inside the app. To have your whole account and its data removed, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Children

PackTrax is built for adults running a business and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
